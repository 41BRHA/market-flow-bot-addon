Trade dates and disclosure dates differ. Reported amounts are ranges. Bounded ranges use their midpoint; open-ended ranges use their disclosed minimum. Market prices use Market Flow's cached close, not the politician's execution price.
Whole filing filters the summed estimate for one politician on one disclosure date. Each transaction keeps only individual trades inside the selected range.
Politician Stock Exposure groups all collected transactions by ticker. “Net disclosed flow” means estimated purchases minus estimated sales inside the selected window. It is not proof of a current holding because opening positions and full/partial sale status are not always known. Prices and max pain refresh in the background about every eight hours.
Value means the summed midpoint estimate of Buy/Sell disclosure ranges. Max pain is the nearest option expiry strike calculated from open interest; unavailable means no usable chain has been cached.

Methods, sources, coverage and CSV import

Trade Score (0–100): 50 is neutral. Buys score positively when price rises; Sells score positively when it falls. Average return and win rate are shrunk toward 50 for small samples.

Exposure values: bounded ranges use their midpoint. Open-ended ranges use their minimum. Net flow can be negative but does not imply a short position. Max pain is an options-derived reference, not a price forecast.

House records come from official Clerk PTR indexes and PDFs. Senate collection needs a configured provider or CSV import. Scanned/unsupported filings and transactions without an explicit ticker may be absent.
